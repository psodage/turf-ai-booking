Last Updated: 1 January 2025

At Turf AI Booking, we strive to provide a smooth and fair experience for every customer. This Refund & Cancellation Policy outlines the conditions under which refunds are processed for cancelled or disrupted turf bookings. By completing a booking and payment, you agree to the terms of this policy.

1. Cancellation by the Customer

Cancellation requests must be submitted by the customer who made the original booking, via one of the following channels:

  • WhatsApp: Message us at +91 74991 62891
  • Email: support@turfaibooking.com with subject line: "Cancellation Request — [Booking ID]"

The time of cancellation is determined by when we receive your cancellation request, not when you send it. Please ensure you receive a confirmation reply acknowledging your cancellation.

2. Refund Eligibility & Cancellation Windows

Refund amounts are calculated based on how much time remains before your booked slot at the time of cancellation:

Cancellation Timing Refund Amount Processing Time
12 hours or more before slot 100% Full Refund 5–7 business days
6 to 12 hours before slot 50% Partial Refund 5–7 business days
Less than 6 hours before slot No Refund —
Failed / duplicate payment 100% Auto-Refund 3–5 business days

3. Non-Refundable Circumstances

The following situations are not eligible for a refund:

  • Cancellation made less than 6 hours before the booked slot time.
  • No-show — customer fails to arrive at the turf without prior cancellation.
  • Violation of Ground Usage Rules resulting in early removal from the premises.
  • Cancellation due to incorrect slot selection by the customer (e.g., wrong date/time) when there is insufficient notice.

4. Cancellation by Turf AI Booking

In rare circumstances, we may need to cancel your booking due to reasons beyond our control, including:

  • Ground maintenance emergencies or infrastructure failure at the turf facility.
  • Extreme weather conditions making play unsafe (e.g., flooding, lightning).
  • Force majeure events — natural disasters, government orders, or civil emergencies.
  • Technical failure of the booking system causing a double booking error.

In all cases of cancellation initiated by us, you will receive a 100% full refund regardless of timing, along with a formal apology notification via WhatsApp. We will attempt to reschedule your slot at no extra cost wherever possible.

5. Refund Processing

All approved refunds are processed through Razorpay back to the original payment method used during booking:

  • UPI (BHIM / PhonePe / GPay): Refunded to the originating UPI ID. Processing time: 3–5 business days.
  • Credit / Debit Cards: Refunded to the card used. Processing time: 5–7 business days depending on your bank.
  • Net Banking: Refunded to the originating bank account. Processing time: 5–7 business days.
  • Wallets (Paytm, etc.): Refunded to the originating wallet. Processing time: 2–3 business days.

Razorpay will initiate the refund within 1–2 business days of our approval. The remaining time is dependent on your bank or payment provider. We have no control over delays on the banking side.

You will receive a refund confirmation via WhatsApp once the refund has been initiated by Razorpay, along with a Refund Reference ID for your records.

6. Failed & Duplicate Payments

  • If your payment was deducted but no booking confirmation was received, this may indicate a payment processing failure.
  • In such cases, Razorpay typically initiates an automatic reversal within 5–7 business days.
  • If you do not receive an auto-refund within 7 business days, contact us immediately via WhatsApp or email with your payment screenshot or UTR number.
  • We will investigate with Razorpay and ensure your full refund is processed within 3 business days of confirmation.

7. How to Request a Refund

To initiate a refund or cancellation, follow these steps:

  1. Contact us via WhatsApp at +91 74991 62891 or email at support@turfaibooking.com.
  2. Provide your Booking ID (from the confirmation message), your name, and the reason for cancellation.
  3. Our team will verify the booking and confirm your refund eligibility within 2 business hours.
  4. If eligible, the refund will be initiated via Razorpay within 1–2 business days.
  5. You will receive a Refund Reference ID on WhatsApp once initiated.

8. Dispute Resolution

If you disagree with our refund decision, you may escalate the matter by:

  • Emailing us at support@turfaibooking.com with subject: "Refund Dispute — [Booking ID]".
  • We will review all disputes within 3 business days and provide a final resolution.
  • If still unresolved, you may contact Razorpay's customer support directly for payment-related disputes.
