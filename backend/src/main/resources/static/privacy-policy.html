Privacy Policy

We value your privacy. This policy explains how Turf AI Booking collects, uses, and protects your personal data.

Last Updated: 1 January 2025

Welcome to Turf AI Booking ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and protect information obtained from you when you access our website at turfaibooking.com or interact with us through WhatsApp Business Cloud API to book football turf slots.

By using our service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of our services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal information when you use our services:

  • Identity Information: Your name as provided during booking.
  • Contact Information: Your WhatsApp-registered mobile number and, if provided, your email address.
  • Booking Information: Date, time, duration, and turf slot preferences.
  • Payment Information: Transaction IDs and payment status through Razorpay. We do not store card numbers, CVV, or UPI credentials.
  • Communication Data: Messages sent and received through our WhatsApp Business account related to bookings and support.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, and operating system collected automatically when you visit our website for security and analytics purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information solely for the following legitimate purposes:

  • To process and confirm your turf booking.
  • To send booking confirmations, reminders, and updates via WhatsApp.
  • To process payments securely through Razorpay payment gateway.
  • To handle cancellations, refunds, and rescheduling requests.
  • To improve our AI assistant and overall service quality.
  • To respond to your support queries and complaints.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. WhatsApp Communication

Our booking service operates primarily through WhatsApp Business Cloud API, provided by Meta Platforms, Inc. When you initiate a conversation with us on WhatsApp:

  • Your WhatsApp number and messages are processed through Meta's infrastructure, subject to Meta's Privacy Policy.
  • Our AI assistant reads your messages to understand booking intent and respond appropriately.
  • We may send automated messages including booking confirmations, payment links, and reminders.
  • You may opt out of marketing messages at any time by replying "STOP" to any WhatsApp message from us.
  • Transactional messages (booking confirmation, payment receipts) cannot be opted out of as they are essential to the service.

4. Payment Processing via Razorpay

All financial transactions are processed through Razorpay Software Private Limited, a PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment gateway. When you make a payment:

  • You are redirected to Razorpay's secure payment interface via a link shared on WhatsApp.
  • Razorpay collects your payment details directly; we never see or store your card or UPI credentials.
  • We only receive a transaction ID and payment status (success/failure) from Razorpay.
  • Your payment data is governed by Razorpay's Privacy Policy, available at razorpay.com/privacy.

5. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Meta (WhatsApp infrastructure), Razorpay (payment processing), and cloud hosting providers — bound by strict data processing agreements.
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or competent government authority.
  • Business Transfer: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, users will be notified beforehand.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide the service and comply with legal obligations:

  • Booking records are retained for 3 years for accounting and dispute resolution purposes.
  • WhatsApp conversation logs are retained for 12 months.
  • Payment transaction records are retained as mandated by Indian financial regulations.

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Our measures include:

  • HTTPS/TLS encryption for all data in transit.
  • Secure cloud infrastructure hosted on Render.
  • Access controls limiting data access to authorized personnel only.
  • Regular security reviews and monitoring.

Despite our best efforts, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We encourage you to protect your own account credentials and notify us immediately if you suspect any unauthorized access.

8. Your Rights

Under applicable data protection laws, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention requirements).
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time.
  • Lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at support@turfaibooking.com.

9. Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses minimal cookies strictly necessary for functionality. We do not use third-party advertising cookies or cross-site tracking. You may disable cookies in your browser settings without affecting the core booking service (which operates via WhatsApp).

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of our service after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy. For significant changes, we will notify you via WhatsApp.
